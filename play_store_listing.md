⏳ CHRONOVAULT — The ultimate history timeline challenge.

Do you know your ancient empires from your industrial revolutions? Can you place the moon landing before or after the transistor? Put your historical knowledge to the test!

HOW TO PLAY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• You're given 6 shuffled historical events from a single category
• Drag and rearrange them into the correct chronological order — oldest to newest
• Hit SUBMIT before the timer runs out
• 3 stars for blazing fast answers, 2 for solid effort, 1 for just making it
• Progress through all 50 rounds to unlock the full vault!
